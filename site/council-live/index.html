What has and has not been tested

  
Tested on a Windows 11 desktop computer, in Microsoft Edge 154 and in Google Chrome 155 (the Chrome for Testing build, not the everyday release). On that computer the check took about 13 to 15 seconds for Wokingham and about 11 to 12 seconds for Bracknell Forest.

  
We also looked up every flagged row's transaction number in the council's file named for it, with a separate program: all 49,300 rows of Wokingham and all 56,723 rows of Bracknell Forest were found as a value in that file.

  
Not tested: phones and tablets, Safari (on a Mac or an iPhone), Firefox, and anything other than Windows. Before it downloads anything the page checks that your browser can give the check the memory it needs (about 0.35 GiB for Wokingham and 0.24 GiB for Bracknell Forest on the computer we tested) and shows the prepared results when it cannot. That check has not been run on a phone or in Safari, so there a run may be refused, may be slow, or may fail in a way we have not seen.

  
What this page does not do. It lists the rows our checks flag in the copies of the council's files we saved. It does not check the council's current files and it does not check our results for you: to check a row, look up its transaction number in the council's file.
